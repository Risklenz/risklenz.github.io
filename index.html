Customer API

Connects your system (core banking, CRM, onboarding app) to RiskLens360. Through it you save customers with their shareholders, directors, UBOs and bank accounts. You also run the risk calculation and close cases, screen names against the watch lists, and download customer reports as PDF.

Everything is saved to the same RiskLens360 database the portal uses. A customer saved through the API shows in the portal like any other: Customer Listing, Customer Cases, Screening Log and Reports.

Base URL: https://<your-server>/api (local: http://localhost:5080/api)
Interactive test page: /swagger. Click Authorize, paste your key, then use Try it out on any call.
Format: JSON in and out (UTF-8). Dates are yyyy-MM-dd.

Authentication

Every call needs the header X-Api-Key. Your RiskLens360 administrator gives you the key.

X-Api-Key: rl_xxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxx
  • A key belongs to one portal user of your company. The customers you save belong to that company, and that user is shown as "saved by" and "decided by".
  • You only ever see the customers, screenings and reports of your own company.
  • Keep the key on your server. Never put it in a web page or a mobile app.
  • A missing or wrong key returns 401.
The administrator creates a key with dotnet run -- create-key <UserLogin> "<client name>". The key is shown only once. Only its hash is stored, and the administrator can switch it off.

Conventions

TopicRule
List fieldsFields such as nationality, idType, pep and occupation take the id (a number) from GET /api/lookups. A field with several values takes an array of ids, e.g. "operationCountries": [255, 435].
Datesyyyy-MM-dd, e.g. "1985-04-21".
Customer {id}The customer id (GUID) or the customer number, e.g. RL9002-1108-03.
All or nothingA save with shareholders, directors, UBOs and banks is checked completely first. If anything is wrong, nothing is saved, not even the customer.
AnswersSuccess: { "success": true, ... }. Error: { "success": false, "error": "..." }.

Errors

StatusMeaning
400A field is missing or wrong. The message lists every problem, e.g. nationality is required; directors[0].ids[0].idType is required.
401Missing or invalid X-Api-Key.
404Customer, screening or report link not found, or not in your company.
409The ID / license number already exists for another customer of your company.
422The risk calculation of a legal customer needs at least one natural or legal shareholder and one director / representative.
500Server error. Try again or contact support.

Typical flow

  1. Load the lists once a day: GET /api/lookups.
  2. Save the customer and run its risk: POST /api/customers/natural?calculateRisk=true (or /legal).
  3. Read the answer. riskLevel / riskScore give the risk. If caseOpen is true, the risk is High or a watch-list match was found, and the customer is waiting for review.
  4. When compliance has reviewed it, clear the case: POST /api/customers/{id}/case/decision with "Onboard" (or "Reject").
  5. Download the reports you need: GET /api/customers/{id}/reports/{type}, which returns a PDF link.

Get a customer

GET/api/customers/{id}

Returns the short customer summary (number, type, name, status, caseOpen) and all details as stored.

Shareholders

There are three kinds, each with its own list and its own add endpoint. The total sharePercent of all shareholders together cannot be over 100. Every kind has sharePercent, typeOfShares, numberOfShares, alias.

KindRequiredOptional
Natural (naturalShareholders)fullName, nationality, dateOfBirthcountryOfResidence, placeOfBirth, occupation, sourceOfFunds, sourceOfWealth (text), pep, phone, email, address, isUbo, isDirector, ids[]
Legal (legalShareholders)legalName, countryOfIncorporationdateOfIncorporation, licenseType, licenseNumber, licenseIssueDate, licenseExpiryDate, businessActivities[], operationCountries[], sourceOfFunds, sourceOfFundsCountries[], phone, email, address
Trust (trustShareholders)trustName, typeOfTrust, jurisdictionOfLawtrustRegistered (true/false), registeredAddress, shareClass (1/2), trusteeName, trusteeType

isUbo / isDirector on a natural shareholder also save the person as UBO / director, as the portal form does.

GET/api/customers/{id}/shareholders
POST/api/customers/{id}/shareholders/natural
POST/api/customers/{id}/shareholders/legal
POST/api/customers/{id}/shareholders/trust

The add endpoints take a list ([ {...}, {...} ]) and are for legal customers already saved. They do not re-run the risk calculation, so call /risk afterwards.

Directors / representatives

GET/api/customers/{id}/directors
POST/api/customers/{id}/directors

Required: fullName, nationality, dateOfBirth. Optional: lastName, alias, countryOfResidence, placeOfBirth, city, phone, email, address, occupation, sourceOfWealth, pep, isCeo, isAuthorisedRep, ids[].

UBOs

GET/api/customers/{id}/ubos
POST/api/customers/{id}/ubos

The same person fields as a director, plus sourceOfFunds, sharePercent, shareholderName. shareholderName is the shareholder the UBO holds through, by its name. It must be a shareholder sent in the same request or one already on file.

Bank accounts

GET/api/customers/{id}/banks
POST/api/customers/{id}/banks

Required: bankName. Optional: accountType (1 Call, 2 Fixed, 3 Current), currencies[], accountNumber, iban, swift, accountDetails, modeOfSignatory, signatories, internetBanking (true/false), alias, openDate, closeDate. Natural and legal customers can both have bank accounts.

ID documents (ids[])

A natural shareholder, a director and a UBO can each have several ID documents:

"ids": [
  { "idType": 1,  "idNumber": "PK7788990",          "idIssueDate": "2022-01-01", "idExpiryDate": "2032-01-01", "idPlaceOfIssue": 253 },
  { "idType": 10, "idNumber": "784-1985-1112223-4", "idIssueDate": "2024-05-01", "idExpiryDate": "2027-05-01", "idPlaceOfIssue": 255 }

idType (list shareholderIdTypes) and idNumber are required in each. The expiry must be after the issue date. The natural customer itself has one ID (idType, idNumber, idIssueDate, idExpiryDate).

Risk calculation

POST/api/customers/{id}/risk

This runs the RBA with the company's current risk settings and screens the customer and its relations against the watch lists. It returns riskScore, riskLevel, screeningHit, screening, factors[] and the new status:

StatusMeaning
1 PendingSaved, risk not calculated yet.
2 Awaiting ReviewHigh risk or a watch-list match. The case is open (caseOpen: true).
3 OnboardRisk accepted, or the case was cleared.
5 RejectedThe case was rejected.

Case

GET/api/customers/{id}/case

Shows the customer status, the parties that need review (matched relations and the customer) with their remarks, and the case activity.

POST/api/customers/{id}/case/review

{ "remarks": "..." } puts one remark on the customer and on all its matched relations. Add "partyId" (from GET .../case) to review one relation only.

Clear or reject the case

POST/api/customers/{id}/case/decision
{ "decision": "Onboard", "remarks": "All matches checked: different person (date of birth differs)." }

decision: Onboard (clear) or Reject. One call does the whole review. The remark is saved on every matched relation (shareholders, directors, UBOs) and on the customer, then the case is closed. Everything is kept in the case activity, as in the portal.

The case should be cleared only after a compliance officer has checked the matches. The remark is the audit trail.

Screening report

GET/api/screening/{screeningId}
GET/api/screening/{screeningId}/report

The first returns the saved screening as JSON. The second returns the report as an HTML page, which can be printed or saved as PDF from a browser.

Customer reports (PDF)

These are the same reports as the portal's Customer Overview. Pass the customer and the report type, and you get a link to the PDF.

typeReportContent
detail (or profile)Customer DetailCustomer profile, shareholders, directors, UBOs with IDs, bank accounts

GET/api/customers/{id}/reports/{type} → link
GET/api/customers/{id}/reports/{type}/pdf → the PDF file
GET/api/customers/{id}/reports/{type}/html → the same report as HTML
